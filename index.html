About USDTF

USDTF is a BEP‑20 experimental token deployed on the BNB Smart Chain. It is used for decentralized finance testing, smart contract interaction, and liquidity pool experimentation. The token operates without any peg, guarantee, or promise of value and is maintained for educational and development purposes only.

Key Features

  • Fully BEP‑20 standard token
  • Active liquidity pool on PancakeSwap
  • Transparent and auditable on-chain transactions
  • Designed for experimentation and DeFi testing
  • No rebasing or supply manipulation mechanisms
